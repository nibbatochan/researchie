Article: GPU Latency Economics in Autoregressive Serving

In foundation model serving architectures, autoregressive decoding is intrinsically bound by memory bandwidth throughput rather than arithmetic tensor compute capabilities. As sequence length increases, KV-cache bandwidth demands scale linearly with batch concurrency.

"Dynamic speculative decoding paired with FP8 block-scaled quantization delivers a 2.4x throughput increase on H100 clusters while cutting per-token energy dissipation by 38%."

Consequently, multi-tenant inference clusters require fine-grained cache paging mechanisms to maximize token generation efficiency.
