Files are the Latin subset (WOFF) from the @fontsource npm packages.
JetBrains Mono files were modified: the 'calt' ligature feature was emptied, because the Latin subset's ligature glyphs are broken and crash PDF rendering (for example on '//' in URLs). Under the OFL a modified font may not use the Reserved Font Name for distribution outside this app; these files are used only inside generated PDFs.
